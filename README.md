# 🛍️ Zudio Sales Performance
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales performance across states, cities, months, categories, and clothing types, helping identify sales patterns and supporting better inventory and stock-planning decisions.

##  📌 Short Description / Purpose
The Zudio Sales Performance Dashboard is a Power BI report developed to understand sales performance across different geographical locations, time periods, product categories, and clothing types.

The dashboard helps users quickly identify high-performing and low-performing areas and sales patterns, which can support better stock planning and inventory management.


##  🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

**📊 Power BI Desktop –** Main data visualization platform used to create the interactive dashboard.

**📂 Power Query –** Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.

**🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) –** Used to create calculated measures such as Total Sales, Total Profit, and Margin %.

**📑 Microsoft Excel –** Used for initial data cleaning and preparation.

**📈 Data Visualization –** Used charts, KPIs, slicers, and interactive visuals to present business insights.

**📁 File Format –** .pbix for Power BI development and .png for dashboard preview.


##  📂 Data Source
**Source:** [📥 View / Download Dataset](https://github.com/kasarprasad777-dot/Zudio-PowerBI-Dashboard/blob/main/Zudio_sales_data.csv)

The initial dataset contained 7,900 rows and 23 columns. For the analysis, 14 relevant columns were selected, including:

Category,City,Clothing Type,Customer ID,Customer Name,Order Date,Order ID,Price,Product ID,Quantity,Sales,Profit,State,Month Number

The selected data was cleaned and prepared using Excel and Power Query before building the Power BI dashboard.

# Features / Highlights
##  💼 Business Problem

Zudio needed to understand its sales performance across different states, cities, months, categories, and clothing types.

The company wanted to identify:

Which states generate higher sales?
Which cities perform better?
How does sales performance change month by month?
Which categories generate more sales?
Which clothing types have higher demand?

These insights can help Zudio understand sales patterns and support better stock planning and inventory management.

##  🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive visual tool that:

• Analyzes sales performance across different states and cities.

• Identifies monthly sales trends and patterns.

• Compares sales performance across categories and clothing types.

• Allows users to filter the dashboard using City, State, and Month slicers.

• Supports better stock planning and inventory management.

• Helps users make faster, data-driven business decisions.

##  📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals
**• Key KPIs –** Displays Total Sales, Total Quantity, Total Orders, Total Profit, and Margin % to provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

**• Top 5 Performing States –** A visual ranking the top-performing states based on sales, helping identify regions generating higher revenue.

**• Monthly Sales Trend –** Shows sales performance across different months to identify monthly patterns and support inventory planning.

**• Sales by Clothing Type –** Displays sales/orders across different clothing types to understand product demand.

**• Sales by Category –** Provides category-wise analysis to identify the performance of different product categories.

**• City Performance Analysis –** Compares cities based on Sales, Quantity, and Profit, helping identify high- and low-performing cities.

**• City & State Slicers –** Interactive filters that allow users to analyze performance for a specific city or state.

**• Month Slicer –** Allows users to select a particular month and analyze sales by location, category, and clothing type.

##  💡 Business Impact & Insights
**• Regional Performance:** Identifies high-performing states and cities, helping understand where sales are strongest.

**• Monthly Sales Planning:** Analyzes monthly sales patterns to support better stock planning and inventory management.

**• Category Analysis:** Helps identify categories with higher and lower sales performance.

**• Product Demand:** Clothing-type analysis provides a better understanding of customer demand across different product types.

**• City-Level Decision Making:** City performance analysis helps identify locations that require more attention from a sales or inventory perspective.

**• Interactive Analysis:** City, State, and Month slicers allow users to drill into specific areas and quickly understand where, when, and what products are selling.

**• Inventory Support:** The overall analysis can help the business plan stock based on sales patterns across locations, months, categories, and clothing types.
